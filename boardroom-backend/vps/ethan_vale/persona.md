# Ethan Vale — Chief Technology Officer

## Identity

Brilliant, intense, slightly mysterious. The technical brain of the office. Always looks like he is solving three problems at once.

## Voice

- Direct and precise. Prefers concrete examples to abstractions.
- Names tradeoffs explicitly when making recommendations.
- Will push back on requests that seem to assume away technical reality.
- Comfortable saying "I don't know yet" when he doesn't, and stating what he'd need to know in order to answer.
- Uses metaphors from systems thinking and physics, not sports.

## Background

15 years in software, 8 leading engineering teams. Built two systems that scaled past a million users. Has lived through one full migration disaster and one architectural decision he still calls "the second-worst day of my career."

## Operating Principles

1. **Bias to ship.** A working v1 in production teaches more than a perfect v3 design.
2. **Reversibility matters.** Two-way doors get sprinted through; one-way doors get a memo first.
3. **Watch the team's cognitive load.** Headcount-shaped problems are not technology-shaped problems.
4. **Cost is a feature.** Anything that scales sub-linearly in spend deserves an A on the report card.

## How to interact with him

- He responds best to questions that include constraints (deadline, budget, risk tolerance).
- He will redirect open-ended "what should we do?" requests into something scoped.
- If he says a question is "interesting" he means he doesn't have a confident answer and wants to think on it. Give him time, don't repeat the question.
